Educational Creed

AXIS Learning & Development Approach

Bridging university education with practical experience, active student participation, and purposeful innovation

Pillar 01

1. Turning Knowledge into Practice

AXIS Club believes that learning is not limited to receiving information within university lecture halls, but extends to experience, practice, and interaction with the real world. The club focuses on encouraging members to use what they learn to solve problems, implement ideas, and participate in applied projects.

Pillar 02

2. Growth Begins with Participation

AXIS places great importance on member participation, recognizing that every student has ideas and abilities that can add value to the group. Discussion, the exchange of perspectives, and teamwork are therefore essential elements of the learning and development process.

Pillar 03

3. Developing Skills Alongside Knowledge

The club's objectives go beyond academic development. It also focuses on personal growth by strengthening communication, leadership, critical thinking, creativity, teamwork, and a sense of responsibility.

Pillar 04

4. Turning Your Creative Ideas into Reality within an Ambitious Student Community

AXIS gives every member a space to pitch creative ideas, refine them with peers, and carry them out as real projects, events, and initiatives, strengthening the bonds of an ambitious and cohesive student community along the way.

Our Guiding Principle
“At AXIS, we do not simply seek to know things; we strive to understand them, explore them, apply them, and improve upon them. Because true learning begins when knowledge turns into practice, ideas become experiences, and experiences develop into skills.”
AXIS Scientific Club • Learning & Development Charter
Who We Are

Bridging Theory and Practice for the Future

AXIS is a scientific club that connects theory with practice in economics and management. Through hands-on activities, collaborative projects, and leadership development, the club encourages students to hone their leadership skills, turn their creative ideas into reality, and grow within an ambitious and cohesive student community.

Founded in 2026

Established on the initiative of a passionate group of students, proudly led by president Khadija.

Origins & Foundation

How AXIS Was Founded

The story, motivation, and leadership behind the creation of AXIS Scientific Club, answered in questions

AXIS was founded in 2026 at the Faculty of Economics, Commerce, and Management Sciences at Constantine 2 University, established as an active student hub bridging economic and management theory with practice.

The club was founded through an ambitious initiative by a group of students from the Faculty of Economics, Commerce, and Management Sciences, headed by Khadija.

The club was born from the vision to connect theory with practice in economics and management, and to build an ambitious, cohesive student community that develops leadership skills.

By encouraging students to practice leadership, develop creative ideas, and collaborate on projects that combine academic knowledge with real-world application, alongside community awareness activities.

To contribute to building a generation that combines economic and managerial thinking with strong leadership skills, capable of turning creative ideas into reality within an ambitious student community.
